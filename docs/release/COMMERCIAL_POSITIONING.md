# Commercial positioning

## Target users

- Universities and network-security laboratories.
- Private-cloud and data-center operators.
- SDN/OpenFlow research teams.
- Managed network-security providers.
- Programmable-network testbeds.

## Differentiator

Explainable detection is connected directly to programmable switch enforcement
and evidence verification. The product explains the decision, shows the path,
installs a time-bounded rule, and proves the action through switch counters.

## Product editions

### Research edition

Local Mininet/OVS/Ryu environment, dashboard, experiments, and documentation.

### Team edition

Authenticated API, persistent event storage, policy management, reports, and
multiple laboratory environments.

### Enterprise edition

TLS device enrollment, high availability, multi-tenancy, physical-device
integrations, SSO/MFA, audit retention, support, and service-level objectives.

## Commercial boundary

The current release is a validated MVP foundation. Enterprise deployment
requires security review, TLS, persistent identity, durable storage, HA,
operational monitoring, and device-specific integration testing.
